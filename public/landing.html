Simple tools for real days

Designed to be practical, not perfect. Map out the broad strokes of your day.

Schedule your day

Create flexible time blocks for routines like mornings, meals, and bedtimes. Keep it high-level to avoid feeling overwhelmed.

Adapt activities

Choose activities for the time, supplies and supervision you have, then adjust your routine when plans change.

Household notes

Keep track of small to-dos or reminders specific to parts of the day, helping you remember the little things.

Your planner stays on this device

We believe your family's routine is your business alone.

KidRhythm is a local-device application. This means there are no user accounts, no passwords, and no cloud synchronization. When you create a schedule, it is saved directly into your browser's local storage.

  • No accounts required to start planning immediately.
  • Planner entries are saved in this browser. Use a separate browser profile on shared devices.
  • Important: Clearing your browser data will erase your saved routines.
