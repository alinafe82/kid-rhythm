Your household, your device

Privacy in plain language.

KidRhythm is for adult parents and caregivers. Your planner is saved in this browser, without a cloud account.

What stays on your device

Child nicknames, age, activity preferences, routine blocks, notes and feedback stay in browser storage. They are not uploaded by the planner. Use nicknames and avoid information you do not need for planning.

A shared device needs care

Browser storage is not encrypted by KidRhythm and this is not a secure online account. Anyone using the same unlocked browser profile may access it. Use your device lock and a separate browser profile. Private browsing, clearing site data or switching devices may remove or hide your plan. There is no cloud recovery or cross-device sync.

Saving, downloads and deletion

The planner tells you if a change cannot be saved. Your device data remains until you remove it or clear this website’s storage in your browser settings. A planner download, if you create one, can contain household details: store it privately and share it only deliberately. KidRhythm does not email it.

Website requests

The hosting provider receives ordinary website requests, including network information such as your IP address. The planner does not load Google Analytics, advertising, third-party fonts or embedded media. Our public source code is available for inspection; your locally entered planner data is not part of that repository.

Advertising status

The public website includes verification for AdSense publisher ca-pub-9595222068473508. Verification is not approval or proof of income. Advertising is not enabled on this release while site approval and the required consent setup remain unverified. Household screens will remain free of advertising and tracking.

Help without sharing private information

Report a software problem on GitHub. Issues are public: never include children’s names, routines, notes, emails or planner downloads. Describe the steps using invented examples.
